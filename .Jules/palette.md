## 2024-09-14 - Visual Context in Network Graph
**Learning:** When users analyze complex visualizations like relationship network graphs, abstract action labels like "그래프 확대" (Zoom In) and "전체 그래프 맞춤" (Fit Graph) can lack immediate affordance.
**Action:** Consistently pair abstract view-manipulation actions with standard recognized icons (like `ZoomIn` or `Maximize` from `lucide-react`) combined with `inline-flex items-center` to immediately communicate functionality and improve the scanability of graph control toolbars.
